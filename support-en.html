Here for your shop

For game issues or privacy requests, email telefoncu.destek@icloud.com.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, the in-game day and steps to reproduce the problem. You may include a screenshot with personal details hidden. Never send passwords, payment card details or verification codes.

Full Version and restoring purchases

The first 5 in-game days are free. Full Version is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Daily Case remains free. If you already purchased, use the same Apple purchasing account and tap “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen. Restoring a purchase does not transfer local game progress. If prices are missing, check your connection and App Store account, then try again.

Payments and refunds

Apple handles payments and refund decisions. For billing issues or refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem page. The developer cannot see your card details or issue refunds directly.

Game saves

Progress is stored on your device. Consider the risk of losing progress before removing the app. Restoring a purchase does not necessarily restore a save. Uninstalling should not be your first troubleshooting step.

Language, sound and notifications

Open Settings using the gear in the upper-right corner of the main menu or counter. Language, sound and reduced motion options are available there. Manage notification permission in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Telefoncu.

Privacy requests

Email support to request access, correction or deletion of personal data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
