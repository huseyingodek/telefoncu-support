Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This policy covers the Telefoncu mobile game and this support website. Developer and data controller: Hüseyin Gödek. Contact: telefoncu.destek@icloud.com.

Your save stays on your device

There is no player account registration. Your shop name, progress, settings, Daily Case results and a local copy of purchase status are stored on your device. These game saves are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Operating system backup settings may apply separately. Removing the app may remove local progress.

Purchases and RevenueCat

The first 5 in-game days are free. Continuing requires a one-time Full Version purchase; Daily Case stays free. Apple App Store processes payments. The developer does not receive card or bank details. RevenueCat is used to validate purchases, manage access and restore purchases. It may process purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, receipts/transaction details, an anonymous app user identifier, and technical information such as device and app versions. This information supports purchase functionality, fraud prevention and purchase/revenue analytics. The app does not attach your shop name or support email to your RevenueCat customer profile.

Advertising and tracking

The game contains no advertising or advertising SDK. The developer does not send gameplay behavior to a separate remote analytics service. RevenueCat purchase analytics are processed as described above. We do not track users across apps for targeted advertising. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Notifications and sharing

With your permission, listing messages and reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable notifications in device settings. No remote push server is used for these notifications. Sharing results is your choice. The sharing service you select processes the card or text under its own privacy policy.

Support and this website

If you email us, we process your email address, message and information you voluntarily include to respond to your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information; never send passwords, payment card details or Apple verification codes. Email and website hosting providers may process technical connection logs to operate and secure their services. Visiting this website does not send us your game saves.

Service providers and international processing

Apple, RevenueCat and email/hosting providers process data within their respective roles. Their services may involve processing outside your country. See the Apple Privacy Policy, RevenueCat Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. The developer does not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain on your device until removed by the app or operating system. Apple and RevenueCat purchase-record retention depends on their processes and applicable legal obligations; uninstalling the app does not delete those records. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests, maintain necessary records and meet legal obligations. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion. Some transaction records may need to be retained by law.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability. You may withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Email telefoncu.destek@icloud.com to make a request. If verification is needed, only necessary information will be requested. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Younger players and changes

The game is not specifically designed for young children; the local age rating displayed by the store applies. Contact support if you believe a child has sent us personal information. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date.
